# SwiftRide — Fair Rides, Smart Bidding

**Your fare, your choice.** SwiftRide is a peer-to-peer ride-hailing app where riders suggest a fare and nearby drivers accept or counter-offer. Transparent pricing, honest driver earnings, and safety built in — starting in **Jhang, Punjab, Pakistan**.

> **2% Platform Fee • 98% to Driver** — a flat, permanent platform fee on every trip.

## Features

### 💬 Smart Fare Bidding
Suggest the fare you think is fair. Nearby drivers can accept it or send a counter-offer — you choose the one that suits you.

### 📍 Precise Pickup & Drop-off
Search any address or landmark, or drop a pin with the interactive center-pin map picker. Routes follow real roads with live ETAs.

### 📊 Transparent Trip Economics
See an estimated fare before you book, based on distance, duration, and vehicle type — with no hidden surcharges.

### ✨ AI Ride Assistant (Optional)
Get help with questions, earnings insights for drivers, and smart safety check-ins. AI features are only enabled with your consent and can be turned off anytime in **Settings › AI Assistant Data Sharing** — all core ride features keep working.

### 🔄 Rider & Driver in One App
Switch between Rider mode and Driver mode from a single account — no second app needed.

### 🌙 Light & Dark Mode
A clean, modern interface that's easy on the eyes day or night, with large touch targets for use on the go.

## How It Works

1. **Set your destination** — Your pickup is detected automatically. Search or pin where you want to go.
2. **Choose your ride** — Pick a Car, Bike, or Rickshaw and set the number of seats you need.
3. **Offer your fare** — Start with the suggested fare or adjust it. Drivers respond in real time.
4. **Ride & arrive** — Track your driver live on the map with safety features on throughout the trip.

## For Drivers — Drive More, Keep More

SwiftRide charges a **permanent 2% platform fee**, so drivers keep **98% of every fare**. The app also shows your real take-home after estimated fuel and vehicle wear.

| Example Breakdown | Amount |
|---|---|
| Agreed fare | PKR 500.00 |
| SwiftRide Service Fee (2%) | − PKR 10.00 |
| **Driver Net Payout (98%)** | **PKR 490.00** |

*Illustrative example. Actual fares are agreed between rider and driver for each trip.*

## Safety First

- **🛡️ SwiftGuard Check-ins** — If a trip stops unexpectedly or goes off-route, SwiftGuard checks in to make sure everything is okay.
- **📱 Verified Phone Sign-in** — Every account is verified with a one-time code.
- **🔒 Privacy by Design** — Data is encrypted in transit and never sold. Delete your account anytime from inside the app.
