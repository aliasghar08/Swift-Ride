## 1. Overview

At SwiftRide (operated by DartNexusLab), we respect your privacy and provide transparent, complete control over your personal data. In compliance with Google Play Store and Apple App Store user data protection guidelines, all users have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

This policy describes:

- The exact categories of data that are permanently deleted.
- What limited data, if any, is retained for legal compliance or fraud prevention.
- The timeline and verification process for complete data removal.

## 2. How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account and personal data using either of the following two methods:

### Method A: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

1. Open the **SwiftRide** mobile application on your device.
2. Sign in to your account.
3. Tap your **Profile avatar** in the top right corner of the home screen to open the Profile menu.
4. Scroll to the bottom of the Profile sheet and tap **Delete Account** (highlighted in red).
5. Read the confirmation warning explaining that this action is permanent and cannot be undone.
6. Tap **Delete My Account Permanently**.
7. Your account credentials, profile, authentication records, and device tokens will be permanently erased immediately, and you will be signed out back to the welcome screen.

---

### Method B: Web or Email Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your mobile device:

1. Send an email to our Data Protection team at: **[aliasgharinnocent@yahoo.com](mailto:aliasgharinnocent@yahoo.com)**

4. To verify ownership and prevent unauthorized deletion of accounts, our team will send a verification SMS or email confirmation to your registered credentials.
5. Once ownership is confirmed, your account and associated personal data will be completely deleted from our production databases within **7 business days**.

## 3. Data That Is Permanently Deleted

Upon receiving and validating your account deletion request, the following information is permanently purged from our servers and storage systems:

- **Authentication Record:** Your user identity (UID), encrypted authentication tokens, and registered phone number.
- **Personal Profile Data:** Full name, display name, email address, profile photo, and user role (`Rider` or `Driver`).
- **Driver Verification Documents (Drivers only):** Vehicle make, model, year, license plate number, government-issued identification cards, and driver license images.
- **Location Data & GPS History:** Saved home and work locations, cached pickup/dropoff coordinates, and driver GPS telemetry.
- **Communication & Push Tokens:** Device push notification registration tokens, customer support chat history, and in-app notifications.
- **Active Offers & Bids:** All pending fare negotiations, ride proposals, and live booking drafts.

## 4. Data Retained and Retention Period

Certain non-identifiable or legally mandated records may be retained for a strictly limited period:

| Data Category | Reason for Retention | Retention Period |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Financial Transaction Records** | Compliance with statutory financial reporting, audit regulations, and tax accounting. | Retained in anonymized audit records for up to 90 days, then permanently purged. |
| **Safety & Fraud Prevention Logs** | Preventing bad actors banned for severe safety violations or fraud from re-registering fresh accounts. | Stored as a one-way cryptographic hash with zero public or marketing access. |

*Note: Retained records are stripped of direct personal contact identifiers and are never used for marketing, advertising, profiling, or user re-engagement.*
