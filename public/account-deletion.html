Account & Data Deletion Policy

SwiftRide (`com.dartnexuslab.swiftride`) • Published by DartNexusLab • Updated November 2026

1. Overview

At SwiftRide (operated by DartNexusLab), we respect your privacy and provide you with complete control over your personal data. In compliance with Google Play Store and Apple App Store user data protection requirements, you may delete your SwiftRide account and all associated personal data at any time.

2. How to Delete Your Account

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the SwiftRide app on your Android or iOS device.
  2. Ensure you are logged into your account.
  3. Tap your Profile avatar in the top-right corner of the Home Screen.
  4. Scroll down to the bottom of the profile sheet and tap Delete Account.
  5. Read the warning prompt and confirm by tapping Delete My Account Permanently.
  6. Your account credentials, profile, authentication records, and device tokens will be permanently erased immediately.

Method 2: Web / Email Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you no longer have the SwiftRide app installed on your smartphone, you can request manual account deletion:

  1. Send an email to: aliasgharinnocent@yahoo.com
  2. Subject: Account Deletion Request - SwiftRide
  3. Provide your registered mobile phone number (with country code, e.g., +92 300 1234567).
  4. Our team will verify ownership via an SMS confirmation code sent to that number.
  5. Upon verification, all account records are permanently purged within 7 business days.

3. Data Categories That Are Permanently Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently purged from our servers (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, and Cloud Storage):

  • User Authentication: Phone number credentials, UID, encrypted authorization tokens.
  • Profile Data: Full name, profile photo, email address, role (Rider or Driver).
  • Driver Verification Records (Drivers only): Vehicle registration, CNIC / ID document images, driver license images, vehicle make and model.
  • Location & GPS History: Saved locations, active driver GPS coordinates, trip telemetry.
  • Device Tokens & Messaging: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens, in-app chat history.
  • Active Offers & Bids: Pending fare proposals and active booking drafts.

4. Data Retention Policy

In accordance with statutory tax and financial accounting requirements in Pakistan:

Data Category Reason for Retention Retention Duration
Financial Transaction Receipts Legal accounting and statutory tax reporting requirements. Retained in anonymized audit tables for up to 90 days, then permanently purged.
Security & Fraud Prevention Logs Preventing banned users from creating unauthorized accounts after serious safety infractions. Restricted one-way cryptographic hash with zero public or marketing access.
