Book a ride in four steps

From opening the app to getting picked up — fast and simple.

Set your destination

Your pickup is detected automatically. Search or pin where you want to go.

Choose your ride

Pick a car, bike, or rickshaw and set the number of seats you need.

Offer your fare

Start with the suggested fare or adjust it. Drivers respond in real time.

Ride & arrive

Track your driver live on the map and enjoy the trip with safety features on.

Drive more. Keep more.

A permanent 2% platform fee means drivers keep 98% of every fare. SwiftRide even shows your real take-home after fuel and wear.
