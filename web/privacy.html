Privacy Policy for SwiftRide

Effective Date: October 17, 2026 • Application: SwiftRide (`com.dartnexuslab.swiftride`) • Developer: DartNexusLab

1. Introduction

DartNexusLab ("we", "our", or "us") operates the SwiftRide ride-hailing and smart bidding mobile application. SwiftRide is a zero-commission platform connecting riders ("Passengers") with verified independent drivers ("Drivers").

This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile applications on Android and iOS. By using SwiftRide, you consent to the data practices described herein.

2. Information We Collect

A. Personal Identification Data

  • Mobile Phone Number: Collected during sign-up and verified via secure Firebase Phone Authentication (SMS One-Time Passwords). It acts as your primary account identifier.
  • Profile Data: Display name, profile image (optional), and chosen account role (Rider or Driver).
  • Driver Verification Info: Vehicle make, model, registration year, color, and license plate number.

B. Location Data (Foreground and Background)

Foreground Location: Captured when the app is in use to pinpoint your pickup point, compute route distance, render maps, and display estimated travel fares.

Background Location (Drivers Only): When a driver goes "Online" or during active trip fulfillment, SwiftRide collects precise GPS coordinates in the background even if the app is minimized. This ensures riders receive live tracking updates, accurate ETAs, and verified route navigation.

C. On-Device AI Pricing Telemetry

SwiftRide includes a local TensorFlow Lite pricing model that suggests sweet-spot biddings based on trip distance, duration, and time of day. All ML inference runs strictly on-device without sending private sensor feeds or sensitive user behavior to external AI servers.

D. Safety & Emergency Data

  • SOS Emergency Alerts: When triggered, records your live coordinates, timestamp, and ride ID to notify emergency dispatch.
  • Trip Sharing: Secure, time-limited web tokens generated when you choose to share your live ride status with trusted contacts.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Facilitating the peer-to-peer ride bidding and matching marketplace.
  • Providing accurate turn-by-turn navigation and map visualizations.
  • Authenticating users via SMS OTP and validating driver boardings via 4-digit PINs.
  • Sending push notifications for counter-offers, ride status changes, and safety updates.

4. Data Sharing and Protection

We do not sell your personal information. Data is only shared between rider and driver for active trip coordination, or with our trusted infrastructure partners (Google Cloud Platform, Firebase, and Google Maps Platform) under strict enterprise confidentiality.

5. Account Deletion & User Rights

You have the right to access, modify, or permanently delete your account and associated personal data at any time. You can request immediate deletion directly in the app settings or by writing to privacy@dartnexuslab.com. All personal records are purged within 30 days.
